## 🚀 How to Use

The application follows a simple process to help you manage your media. 

### Importing Videos
Open the main window and click the folder icon. Select the folder where you keep your downloaded Instagram Reels. The program scans your files and prepares them for the database. If you have not downloaded your videos, use your browser to save them to your machine first.

### Processing
Click the Start button. The program looks at every video. It creates a transcript of the speech inside the video. It saves this text into a local index. This process happens entirely on your machine. No data leaves your computer. The time this takes depends on how many videos you have and how fast your processor is.

### Searching
Type a word or phrase into the search box. The app shows every video that contains that term. Click any result to play the video. You see the transcript on the side of the screen.

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this app use my internet connection?
The app only needs the internet to update itself. It does not send your videos or your transcripts to any server.

### What if the app fails to read a video?
Ensure your video file is in a standard format like MP4. If a file uses a strange format, the program might struggle to play it. Most Instagram downloads work without issues.

### Can I delete the database?
Yes. You can clear the index from the settings menu. Deleting the index does not remove your actual video files.

### Where does the program save my data?
The program keeps the database in a folder inside your Documents directory. You can move this folder to a different drive if you need more space.

## 🔒 Privacy
Your videos belong to you. This software respects that boundary. All transcription happens inside your system. You do not need to sign in with an Instagram account or provide personal keys. Your search history stays on your computer.

## 📝 Troubleshooting
If the program does not open, check your antivirus settings. Sometimes security software blocks new programs. You can add the program folder to your exclusion list to fix this. If you run out of space, the program notifies you. Move your video files to a drive with more capacity and point the program to that new location.
